# Desktop Creatures — POI Art Backlog

A running art/design backlog for Points of Interest in the desktop ecosystem.

The goal is to make POIs reusable across creatures wherever possible rather
than designing everything around a single species.

## Creature Preferences — Future

Creatures should eventually be able to prefer different POIs rather than
choosing purely by distance.

Examples:

- One rat may strongly prefer cardboard boxes.
- Another may prefer tunnels.
- Some creatures may favor particular sleeping locations.
- Personality/friendship could influence POI choices.
- Creatures may develop favorite individual objects.
